# Yusuf Aytas

I engineer systems and lead teams that make execution simpler. My work sits across cloud platforms, data, reliability, distributed systems, and technical leadership. Over the last 20+ years, I have built backend and platform systems, led multi-region engineering organizations, scaled teams across regions, and worked on the operational layer that makes software dependable under real load.

I care about the parts of engineering that are easy to describe badly and hard to execute well: ownership, interfaces, delivery systems, production change, resilience, and the trade-offs behind architecture.

## What I work on

- Distributed systems and backend platforms
- Cloud, data, and reliability engineering
- Developer workflows and operational tooling
- Engineering leadership, execution, and organizational design

## What I write about

I write about engineering as a system. That includes architecture, technical debt, planning, delivery, operational discipline, and team effectiveness. Most engineering problems look local at first. In practice, they are often coordination problems wearing technical clothes.
